Jam Reborn has moved to JR

Jam Reborn is no longer supported and won't connect to Animal Jam any more. Everything it did, and a lot more, is now in JR: profiles for two accounts at once, a trading helper with item values, shop templates, themes and new plugins.

Download JR Join the JR Discord
How to switch
  1. Download and run JR-Setup.exe from the JR page.
  2. Join the JR Discord, then sign in to JR with Discord.
  3. Uninstall Jam Reborn from Windows Settings → Apps.
